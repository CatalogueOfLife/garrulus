Ophelia yangi sp. nov. (Annelida, Opheliidae), a new species from Chinese waters, with comments on the taxonomic characters of the genus

Ophelia limacina (Rathke, 1843), originally described from Norway, is the only species of Ophelia Savigny, 1822 recorded from Chinese waters, but these records have never been verified.

Introduction

Ophelia Savigny, 1822 (Opheliidae Malmgren, 1867) currently comprises 37 valid species (Read and Fauchald 2026).

In Chinese waters, only one species of Ophelia, O. limacina (Rathke, 1843), has been reported.
